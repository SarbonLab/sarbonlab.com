Services

What we do in Kommo

pipelines
Pipelines and fields

We turn your sales process into pipeline stages. We set up lead and contact fields, tags, user roles and access rights.

channels
WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram in Kommo

We connect your channels to Kommo. Chats from all of them land in one inbox, each linked to its lead.

automation
Salesbot and automations

We build no-code Salesbot flows that reply to clients, collect their details, set tasks and move leads through the pipeline.

integrations
Integrations via API, webhooks and n8n

We connect Kommo to your website forms, spreadsheets and internal tools through the Kommo API and webhooks. Multi-step workflows run on n8n.

migration
Migration from spreadsheets or another CRM

We export your data, remove duplicates, map the fields and import contacts, companies and leads into Kommo.

training
Team training

We record video walkthroughs on your own leads and leave a short written guide for new hires.

Process

How we work

Four steps. You approve the scope and the price before we touch your account.

01

Brief. You tell us what you sell, how many people work with leads and which channels the leads come from. By WhatsApp or email.

02

Scope and quote. We send a written list of what will be set up, with a price and a timeline. Work starts after you approve it.

03

Setup. We build everything in your Kommo account. You can open the account at any time and see the progress.

04

Handover. We send your team a recorded walkthrough of the account, answer their questions in writing and hand over the guide.

Questions

FAQ

Do I need a Kommo account before we start?
No. You can start with the 14-day free trial on kommo.com, or we can arrange a subscription for you. If you already have an account, we work in it.
How much does a Kommo licence cost?
As listed on kommo.com, November 2026: Base $25, Advanced $35 and Pro $45 per user per month, billed for 6 months. Enterprise pricing is custom. Kommo bills in USD and the minimum subscription period is 6 months. If we arrange the subscription, you pay the same price as on kommo.com.
How much does the setup cost and how long does it take?
It depends on the scope: the number of pipelines, channels and integrations. Tell us what you need. We send a written scope with a price and a timeline before any work starts.
Can you connect Kommo to a tool that has no ready-made integration?
Yes, if the tool has an API or can send webhooks. We use the Kommo API and webhooks, and build multi-step workflows in n8n.
How do we communicate?
In English and in writing: WhatsApp and email. We work remotely from the UTC+5 time zone and send recorded walkthroughs instead of live calls.
Do you work with amoCRM as well?
Yes. Kommo is the international version of amoCRM. SarbonLab is a certified Kommo partner and an official amoCRM partner in Kazakhstan.
